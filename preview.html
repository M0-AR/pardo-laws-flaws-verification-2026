📖 Glossary (hover-free, plain words)

Backtest
Replaying the recipe on old prices to see what would have happened.
Overfitting
Memorizing old noise instead of learning — brilliant on homework, lost on exams.
Walk-forward
Study 4 years → unseen exam year, repeated while rolling through history; only exams count.
PROM
Profit after deleting your luckiest trade, per dollar risked. The honest score.
Slippage
The gap between the price you saw and the price you got. We overstate it on purpose.
Plateau
Good settings should have good neighbours. A lonely spike is a trap (ours: 0.00 — noted honestly).
WF efficiency
Exam profit ÷ homework profit. QQQ: 0.23. Negative = memorized, not learned.
